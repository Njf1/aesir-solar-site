## What remains operationally broken

The application service should leave Aesir with a complete installation record connected to a verified payment and a job they can act on. The audited site has not yet proved that durable chain; richer explanation and a working local visual handoff do not repair it.

- **A paid application:** this should look like one recoverable set of installation details tied to the correct £300 GBP payment. What is broken now is the missing proved durable link between the full form, stored application and payment identity. Form navigation and mocked route checks alone do not establish that record.
- **A repeated payment callback:** this should leave one correctly paid work item, even if the provider retries. What is not yet proved is authenticated amount/currency/status binding and idempotent fulfilment. A success screen must not stand in for that transaction evidence.
- **A generation display:** this should identify the measurement date and freshness, or clearly show historical/unavailable data. The new section supplies a dated sample; it does not turn the older stale “Today” fallback into a current feed or prove telemetry failures are handled.

Keep authoritative suitability checks, payment/restore/retry/cancellation, trustworthy success/receipt state, provider-fallback handoff, no-JS form behaviour, callback trust and cache/payment boundaries in the independent repair scope. Complete those with appropriate operational evidence before public rollout; do not erase them when marking this editorial milestone delivered.
