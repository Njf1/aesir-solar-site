## UK connection and service boundaries

NGED's G99 procedures distinguish application categories and place Form A1-2 within its Small Generation Installation routes; commissioning tests belong to the installer. Its storage guidance reinforces that the whole proposed arrangement matters. UKPN separates application, connection assessment/offer, potential network work, connection and testing. These sources **do not establish that the illustrated campus or Premier Composites qualifies for A1-2**. No numerical threshold, engineering-recommendation revision, universal cost or turnaround is adopted.

Aesir's **£250 fee + £50 VAT = £300 total per suitable application** is the supplied offer, not an independently audited operational claim. Installers retain their work/accreditation; property owners can discuss installation separately. Physical commissioning and network works remain outside the application fee; the offered commissioning-notification paperwork follows the customer's report of commissioning. Suitability/contact access remains available before payment.
