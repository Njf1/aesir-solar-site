## Art direction review notes

- Judge the current Sun at the 30–39% close-up (the old first-draft hold was 60–80%). It should read as an immense luminous body with structured plasma at several scales, a bright but legible edge and deep space around it. A uniform orange surface, flat noise, oversized bloom or repeated cartoon flame loops fails the visual target.
- The guide needs to remain concentrated light: small white-gold core, softer taper, restrained trail. Reduce the core’s visible diameter before adding more glow; avoid a glass-marble silhouette. Solar flares belong to the Sun and should not all follow the guide.
- Protect the same framing intent on portrait screens by reframing the limb and copy, not shrinking an entire desktop composition. Keep copy and persistent application action outside the brightest region. The delivered regional and roof chapters use separate portrait camera distances and guide-aware aims. Retain those safe areas when extending the glass shot.
- The most important later editorial cut is cell absorption. Ending the photon guide and introducing a distinct current cue keeps the educational claim honest while preserving continuity of energy.
- The service bridge must resolve the spectacle into a concrete £300 application service. It must be readable before WebGL readiness, and reachable with skip, keyboard, reduced motion and no JavaScript. No imagery should imply the fee purchases panels, installation or guaranteed approval.
- The preview establishes visual direction; it does not repair the audited intake/payment chain. Public homepage replacement remains blocked until a validated durable application, stable application/order identity, verified amount/currency/status, idempotent notifications and an actionable fulfilment record are proved together.
