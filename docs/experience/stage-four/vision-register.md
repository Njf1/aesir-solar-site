## Confirmed service copy versus proposed benefit copy

The supplied brief and unchanged business pages establish: **G99 Form A1-2 application service; Great Britain; £250 + £50 VAT = £300 total once per application; preparation, submission and follow-up; no approval guarantee; installers retain their job/accreditation; property owners may separately discuss installation.** These are the preserved proposition and contracts. They are not independent verification of company details, regulation, payment fulfilment or operational performance.

Use `/apply.html`, `/index.html#check` and `/contact.html` for the three primary actions, retaining their existing extensionless equivalents. The form preserves company, contact, email, phone, accreditation, address, postcode, MPAN, inverter, ENA type-test reference, output kW, phases, storage kWh, commissioning date, G100, EPS, notes and both consent checks. There is no new `/start-application` route or generic replacement lead form.

Energy independence, economics, longevity, sustainability, resilience and monitoring are **required future topic coverage**, not confirmed marketing claims. A broad topic must remain in the plan even when its claim evidence is missing. Never fill an evidence gap with stock testimonials, fabricated savings, unsupported warranties or a fictional customer attached to the illustrative site.
