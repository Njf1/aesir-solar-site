## Rendering contract

The scene should make one clear change in meaning: **the white-gold light ends where the silicon absorbs it; a distinct electrical-energy illustration then begins.** The light's energy excites charge carriers already associated with the semiconductor. The cell structure enables separation and current extraction. Generation continues with illumination and a connected circuit. [DOE cell basics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-photovoltaic-cell-basics) and [DOE PV primer](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/articles/pv-cells-101-primer-solar-photovoltaic-cell).

1. **Approach:** keep the existing incident-light guide outside the module while the camera crosses the glass reflection. A narrow sectional reveal exposes the protective layers and silicon; preserve physical continuity with the same module.
2. **Absorb:** the guide reaches a specific point within the silicon, contracts, and disappears there. A restrained local response spreads through a small area of the absorber. Avoid a travelling white-gold head continuing into the contact.
3. **Separate and collect:** reveal a thin structural junction/collection band and two subdued collection overlays within the cell. Their direction toward different collection regions is schematic; these are energy-flow overlays, not named electron or hole markers. Fade them into a broad contact glow, rather than tracking one marker from cell to business.
4. **Extract:** show a low-intensity directional energy-route overlay across metal contacts, through module interconnections and the junction box, and along a plausible paired DC cable to a generic inverter. Keep continuing illumination apparent through a soft incident-light wash or a few restrained repeated light cues. The one highlighted event is an example within continuing illumination.
5. **Convert:** allow the inverter to become the focal object. Label the two sides in accessible HTML, **DC input** and **AC output**. Show the waveform in a small separate explanatory inset, then continue the existing application journey.

These phases guide the implemented deterministic sequence. Derive their opacity, position and layer separation from normalized chapter progress so reverse scrolling reconstructs the same state without overshoot or event history. Reverse scrolling replays the explanation; it is not a claim that the physical conversion reverses. A reduced-motion still can show the exposed stack, the absorption point, collection contacts and a quiet energy-route indication together, with the same HTML explanation.

## Electrical-route and waveform rules

Use a separate visual language for the electrical route: for example a broad, cool-neutral contact highlight with a slow directional modulation. Its purpose is to indicate the route of electrical energy. Its speed is not electron drift speed, a signal delay, current magnitude or a physical parcel of energy. Do not title it “electrons” unless the presentation intentionally represents charge movement with the correct direction and a clear distinction from conventional current.

A paired conductor route and two collection sides make the connected-circuit requirement legible. This need not become a detailed installation schematic. Keep the module interconnection, junction box and inverter connection generic; do not add invented protective-device ratings, export controls, switching sequences or approval states.

The AC inset should be an **original graph of voltage against time**, anchored beside the inverter, with a horizontal zero line and a sinusoidal trace crossing above and below it. Label axes **Voltage** and **Time**. A still clean curve is sufficient; a moving marker or reveal may clarify reading order. An optional qualitative DC line remains on one side of zero. Do not imply actual PV input voltage is perfectly constant under all conditions. [DOE inverter basics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/solar-integration-inverters-and-grid-services-basics) describes DC-to-AC conversion and explicitly explains the sine wave as voltage variation over time.

Use no numeric amplitude, frequency, phase count, input/output rating or control mode. Do not wrap the sine wave around the outgoing wire, turn the cable into a wave, make charge particles follow a sinusoidal path, or imply that one displayed waveform describes the actual building's installation. The inset illustrates a principle; it is not live telemetry.
