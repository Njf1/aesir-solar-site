# Recorded motion review — stage seven

The initial review covered 216 selected video frames in 24 chronological contact sheets: laptop 1280×720 and mobile 390×844, covering all nine changed slow-pass joins forward and backward, two forward passes with copy hidden, and one moving-machinery phase per viewport. These are desktop Chromium recordings, not physical-device footage. This is a sampled-frame/trace review; I did not watch or inspect every video frame and do not infer frame-time performance from 25 fps recording output.

**No visible clipping, overlapping chapter copy, detached optical head/trail or reversed-scroll discontinuity was found in these samples.** One localized readability limitation remains: the dark module-underside connection is hard to follow before the stronger blue DC route appears. In the laptop video, compare 218.28–218.92 s and 274.96 s (copy hidden) with 219.88 s/276.08 s. In the mobile video, compare 188.28–188.92 s and 244.96 s with 189.84 s/246.08 s. The junction and connecting trace are present but very low contrast against the underside; this can briefly make the energy handoff feel less connected. This is a visual clarity observation, not evidence of a blank render, lost asset or geometric discontinuity. The pre-CELL_EXIT darkness is structural underside occlusion and should remain distinct from the avoidable modulation of the DC source cue after exit.

The initial video evidence above predates that correction; the final follow-up below verifies its appearance.
