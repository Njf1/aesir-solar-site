## Confirmed service copy versus proposed benefit copy

The supplied brief and unchanged business pages establish: **G99 Form A1-2 application service; Great Britain; £250 + £50 VAT = £300 total once per application; preparation, submission and follow-up; no approval guarantee; installers retain their job/accreditation; property owners may separately discuss installation.** These are the preserved proposition and contracts. They are not independent verification of company details, regulation, payment fulfilment or operational performance.

Use `/apply.html`, `/index.html#check` and `/contact.html` for the three primary actions, retaining their existing extensionless equivalents. The form preserves company, contact, email, phone, accreditation, address, postcode, MPAN, inverter, ENA type-test reference, output kW, phases, storage kWh, commissioning date, G100, EPS, notes and both consent checks. There is no new `/start-application` route or generic replacement lead form.

Energy independence, economics, longevity, sustainability, resilience and monitoring are **required future topic coverage**, not confirmed marketing claims. A broad topic must remain in the plan even when its claim evidence is missing. Never fill an evidence gap with stock testimonials, fabricated savings, unsupported warranties or a fictional customer attached to the illustrative site.

## Useful stage-three regression invariants

These invariants guided the added tests and browser inspection. Exact executed counts, measured limitations and artifacts are recorded in [the stage-three handoff](README.md). The original regression coverage is retained.

| Invariant | Meaningful verification |
| --- | --- |
| Existing pacing survives named ranges | Save stage-two pose fixtures in the existing three framings. At old local progress `p`, the same pose occurs after `p × 5.6 × viewportHeight` useful scroll from the legacy sequence start, within explicit tolerance. Later chapter length cannot rescale that span. Named ranges are ordered, positive and gap-free; both directions resolve boundary ownership deterministically. Update browser helpers to target a named range plus local progress, not a percentage of the new whole document. |
| One geographic convention | GeoJSON input positions are **[longitude, latitude]** in degrees. Test the chosen conversion independently of its implementation: poles, equator, ±180° seam and known northern-European landmarks; round trips use wrapped longitude comparisons. Under the current globe's SphereGeometry plus Y=π/2 orientation, Greenwich/equator is −Z, 90°E is −X, 90°W is +X and north is +Y. Derive fixtures from this actual texture transform, not a second copy of the helper's formula. [GeoJSON position standard](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7946#section-3.1.1). |
| Geography agrees with the picture | A projected landmark, coastline overlay, camera target and day-map location agree in a rendered inspection. Polygon holes/islands survive simplification; selecting UK political boundaries cannot silently include Northern Ireland in the Great Britain service highlight. Record the exact dataset, terms and preprocessing. [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). |
| Handoffs preserve the view | At Earth→regional and regional→site joins, compare projected feature/guide positions, apparent scale and camera forward/up vectors immediately before/after; transform camera, aim, retained objects and lights by the same basis/origin change. Test C0 and intended C1 in comparable coordinates. A motivated occluded scale change should be verified in captures, not declared smooth because unrelated raw coordinates differ little. |
| Light remains coherent | The Earth Sun direction still matches `−EARTH_POSITION`. Transform that direction through the regional/site basis; test angular agreement and the correct visible day/limb side. Building and panel highlights/shadows must not flip when origins change or while reversing. Artistic fill light, if used, is a separate explicit source. |
| Camera and guide keep clearance | Sample every transition densely in landscape, portrait and short framing. Check camera distance against Earth/terrain, then clearance from roof/module geometry; check look direction is finite, near/far planes remain valid and focal subjects remain inside the intended safe frame. Account for scene scale when setting tolerances. The visible guide must stay ahead of the camera and attach to any continuing trail. |
| Resources stay bounded in both directions | Record renderer, draw/triangle, geometry, texture and decoded-memory counts after warmup. Traverse Earth→site→panel→Earth repeatedly: one canvas/renderer, stable resource counts after initial loads, no duplicate asset requests and bounded panel instances. Unload/disposal cancels owned requests/timers, closes bitmaps, and cannot resurrect a removed chapter after late completion. Measure transfer and decoded/GPU estimates separately. |
| Delayed, failed and reduced-motion regional/site assets | Delay each next-chapter load independently: retain a truthful composed view and matching DOM copy until ready; complete without requiring another scroll. Fetch/decode/warmup each has a bounded failure path. Failure preserves service access. Reduced motion offers meaningful still views through every delivered chapter; selecting a delayed still updates once ready without starting ambient motion. |
| Access and conversion survive longer content | Check persistent Apply and visible Skip before readiness, at every handoff and on fallback. Verify keyboard focus, Back, hash restoration, pause, hidden/offscreen work, 200% text and browser zoom, narrow portrait and short landscape. Copy cannot overlap the focal subject or controls. No-JS content still explains the service, full price and route choices. New assets/bundles stay absent from original form/legal/contact pages. |
| Business contracts and claims remain honest | Retain baseline page/API/form parity tests and mocked payment tests, with all unexpected external calls blocked. Add explicit labels/status review for illustrative building, future chapters, conditional benefits and after-permission operation. Do not let a new “proof” panel silently adopt the example building, old telemetry or an invented testimonial. No test submits a real form, creates a cart/order, emails or deploys. |
