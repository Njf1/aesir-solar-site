BEFORE YOU APPLY

The essentials.

What does the £300 cover?

£250 fee + £50 VAT = £300 total for one suitable G99 Form A1-2 application across Great Britain: preparation, submission and follow-up. Installation, commissioning, network works and approval are separate. Read the refund policy.

What will I need?

Your contact and site details, inverter model, registered AC output and supply information. Include available type-test, storage, export-limitation and backup details. The application form guides you through them.

Can I keep my own installer?

Yes. Installers keep their job and accreditation. Property owners can discuss installation separately with Aesir; it is not included in the application fee.

Is approval or timing guaranteed?

No. The network operator’s assessment and any further requirements determine the outcome and timing. The application-service fee does not grant permission to operate. Talk to us about your installation.
