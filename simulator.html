Measured 24 August 2026 — 14 hours of daylight, 7% cloud, 15°C. Peak hour reached 29.0 kWh, which is the 30 kW inverter's ceiling: this array is clipping at midday. That is exactly the kind of thing a brochure never shows you, and exactly what we design around.

Figures are DC measured at the modules by Tigo Energy's monitoring platform, and typically read 2–5% above the inverter's AC output. The 4.10 kWh attributed to optimisation is Tigo's own modelled estimate of energy recovered from shading and mismatch, not a directly measured quantity. Monitoring data courtesy of Tigo Energy.

Where these numbers come from

  • Yield — PVGIS 5.3 (SARAH3, 2005–2023), Lincoln 53.2307 −0.5406, 14% system loss. South pitched 35° 1,015 kWh/kWp; flat south 10° 908; flat east–west 10° 824. A given year varies about ±7%.
  • Module geometry — 2278 × 1134 mm, 580 W, 22.45% (Jinko Tiger Neo datasheet, Nov 2025). Flat roofs assume landscape ballasted modules at 10°.
  • Electricity price — DESNZ Quarterly Energy Prices, June 2026 (Q1 2026 data), including CCL and excluding VAT.
  • Export — 8p/kWh planning assumption. Ofgem's SEG Year 5 report shows a realised blend of 12.9p, but tied tariffs pay roughly 3.3× untied ones, so 8p is deliberately cautious.
  • Installed cost — DESNZ Solar PV cost data, May 2026 for 10–50 kW; larger bands extrapolated. Great British Energy's published 2025 rooftop projects ran to a median £1,766/kW, so treat the upper end of each band seriously.
  • Degradation — 0.5%/yr, the median for crystalline silicon (Jordan et al., Progress in Photovoltaics, 2016). One inverter replacement is modelled at year 13.
  • Carbon — 0.131 kgCO₂e/kWh, DESNZ conversion factors 2026, Scope 2 location-based.
  • Tax — the Annual Investment Allowance gives 100% relief on the first £1m. Solar is special-rate expenditure, so full expensing does not apply. Figures shown are pre-tax; talk to your accountant.
  • No price escalation is assumed. Savings are held at today's unit rate for 25 years, which is deliberately conservative.
